Features

Everything in one report

Upload a Word document, PowerPoint or PDF. In a few seconds you get a paragraph-by-paragraph analysis, a calibrated range and a list of anything suspicious hiding in the file.

1 · Writing analysis

Where the AI-written part is

Paragraph scores

Text is split into paragraphs of 50–220 words. Each is scored separately and highlighted, so mixed essays are easy to read.

Calibrated 98% range

The AI share comes with a range calibrated by conformal prediction on essays the model never saw. Longer texts get narrower ranges.

Clear verdicts

Likely human, mostly human, mixed, mostly AI, likely AI, or inconclusive. Never a confident answer without the evidence for it.

2 · Traces

The tricks that leave fingerprints

Each finding is labelled strong, medium or weak, so an innocent invisible character never counts as much as a hidden instruction to a grader.

3 · Files and privacy

Works with the files students actually submit

.docx, .pptx, .pdf, .txt

Word, PowerPoint (including speaker notes and hidden slides), text PDFs and plain text, up to 10 MB. Or paste text directly.

Nothing stored

Files are read in memory and discarded. No database of student work, no training on your uploads.

Fair to English learners

Training data includes essays by English-language learners, so plain or imperfect English is not mistaken for AI.
