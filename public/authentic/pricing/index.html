Pricing questions

What counts as a check?

One uploaded file or one pasted text, of any length up to 10 MB.

Is there a limit during beta?

Fair use only: up to 40 checks per hour from one network. Contact us if you need more for a whole class.

Can our university calibrate the model on its own essays?

Yes. With your permission, anonymised essays written before 2020 can be used to calibrate the model to your students’ writing, which narrows the ranges and lowers false alarms.

Do you offer discounts for schools in developing regions?

Yes. Get in touch and tell us about your school.
