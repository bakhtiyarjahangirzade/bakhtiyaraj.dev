Questions? Answers.

Can Authentic prove that a student used AI?

No tool can. Authentic shows evidence and says how sure it is. Hidden instructions to graders are close to proof. A high AI share is a reason to talk with the student, not a verdict.

Why a range instead of one number?

A single number hides its own uncertainty. Our ranges are calibrated so that 98 in 100 contain the true share of AI-written text, and we check this on essays the model never saw.

Do you keep my files?

No. Files are read in memory, analysed and discarded when your report is sent. We do not build a database of student work and never train on uploads.

Which languages are supported?

English. The model is trained on English writing by native speakers and English learners.

Can students use it?

Yes. Students can check their own drafts and see which paragraphs might be questioned. We do not offer tools that rewrite AI text to avoid detection.
