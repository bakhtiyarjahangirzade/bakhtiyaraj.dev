How it works

Three layers of evidence in one report

A single “AI score” is easy to fool and easy to misread. Authentic combines three independent kinds of evidence and tells you how sure it is.

Paragraph analysis

A detector trained on human and AI essays scores every paragraph, so a mixed essay shows exactly where the AI-written part starts.

98% confidence range

Instead of “73% AI” we say “55–91% AI, 98% confidence”. The range is calibrated on held-out essays and gets narrower as the text gets longer.

Trace and file forensics

Hidden or white text, prompt injections aimed at graders, ChatGPT citation leftovers, invisible characters and suspicious editing metadata.

Features

Paragraph highlights

Every paragraph gets its own score and colour, so you can talk with the student about specific passages.

Honest uncertainty

If the evidence is weak, Authentic says “inconclusive” instead of guessing. That is how it keeps false accusations rare.

FAQ

Questions teachers ask

Can Authentic prove that a student used AI?

No tool can. Authentic gives evidence with a stated confidence. Strong traces such as hidden instructions to graders are close to proof; a high AI share is a reason for a conversation, not a verdict.

Why a range instead of one percentage?

Any single number hides how uncertain it is. Our ranges are calibrated so that 98 out of 100 contain the true share of AI text, which we verify on essays the model never saw.

Do you keep the files?

No. Files are processed in memory and discarded when the report is sent back. We do not build a database of student work.

Which languages are supported?

English for now. The model is trained on English essays by native and non-native writers.

Can students use it?

Yes. Students can check their own drafts and see which parts might be questioned. We do not offer tools that rewrite AI text to evade detection.
