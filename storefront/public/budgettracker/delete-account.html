Delete your KuryeBro account or data

You can make this request from the website or Android application. No Android phone is required.

  1. Sign in to KuryeBro. If necessary, use “Forgot password?” first.
  2. Finish any active work shift. Download a full backup from Settings if you want to keep a copy.
  3. Open Settings → Account and data → Delete account or data.
  4. Enter your current password and type DELETE.
  5. If your login is exclusive to KuryeBro, you can also select “Also permanently delete my login account”. Confirm with “Permanently delete”.

Deleting data permanently removes your KuryeBro entries, receipts, routes, daily totals and goals from the active server. Old offline installations are prevented from uploading those erased records again. Wait for the success message; an error means the operation may need to be retried.

If your login is shared with another application, only KuryeBro data is removed. Other applications and the shared login remain. The last administrator login cannot be deleted. A small account reset marker is retained while a shared login exists to prevent erased data from returning.

Downloaded backups are under your control and must be deleted separately. Provider operational logs and infrastructure backups may remain for their retention periods. See the privacy notice.

If you cannot sign in or complete deletion, use the contact options on the developer's website to request help. Never share your password.

Hesabı və məlumatları silmək

Saytda və ya tətbiqdə hesabınıza daxil olun → Ayarlar → Hesab və məlumatlar → Hesabı və ya məlumatları sil. Cari şifrəni daxil edin və DELETE yazın. Ortaq giriş varsa, yalnız KuryeBro məlumatları silinir. Əvvəlcə ehtiyat nüsxəni endirin və aktiv növbəni bitirin. Android telefonu tələb olunmur.
