KuryeBro privacy notice

Updated 19 November 2026 · Developer: Bakhtiyar Jahangirzade

Data used by the application

KuryeBro stores your email and authentication details, nickname, chosen currency, earnings, expenses, goals, daily work totals and any receipt photographs you choose to attach. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; the application does not store a readable copy of your password.

Location is recorded only after you start a work shift and grant permission. Android displays a foreground notification while the route service runs, including when the screen is locked. Finishing the shift stops recording. Route points, distance and elapsed time are stored on your device and synchronised to your account when you are online. The latest shift position can also be available to authorised KuryeBro administrators.

Storage and access

The web application is hosted on Vercel. Authentication, account records and private receipt storage use Supabase. Connections use HTTPS. Records are restricted to their owner; authorised KuryeBro administrators can view courier records, receipts and routes for the application's management features. OpenStreetMap tiles are requested when maps are displayed; the map service receives ordinary network request information, including IP address and requested map tiles.

Offline records, photos and route journals remain on your device. Downloaded JSON backups contain personal financial and location data; keep them private. The application does not sell your data or include advertising tracking.

Retention and deletion

Account records remain until you delete them. You can download a full backup and request immediate deletion from Settings → Account and data. Deletion removes the application's entries, receipt objects, routes and goals. A reset marker is retained with a shared login to prevent an old offline installation from uploading erased records again. Existing downloaded backups and copies on devices outside the application's control are not remotely erased.

Some accounts use the same login as other applications on bakhtiyaraj.dev. KuryeBro deletion preserves those applications and the shared login. A standalone login can also be deleted; the last administrator account is protected from deletion. Infrastructure providers may retain operational logs or backups according to their own service retention policies.

Your controls

You can deny or revoke location and camera permissions in Android settings, stop a shift, export your records, restore a backup, reset your password, and delete your account data. Revoking location permission prevents route tracking; financial records remain usable.

For a privacy request or difficulty accessing your account, use the contact options on the developer's website. Do not send passwords or complete financial backups in a public message.
