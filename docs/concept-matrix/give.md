# Bir Süfrə — community kitchens food charity, Bakı (concept `give`, kind `site`)

**Previous problem.** Showroom template: two-line hero, "impact" counters presented like real statistics, three cards and an AI-generated photo.

**Thesis.** Say plainly what happens with the money. Big, warm, blunt Rubik type on saffron and aubergine bands, produce photographed straight from the crate, and arithmetic instead of slogans: what one food box contains and what it costs (sample figures, labelled), what a donation covers, and which kitchen shift still needs hands. No impact counters.

- **Audience.** Baku residents who want to give money or time; people who need food and must find how to ask.
- **Primary tasks.** Donate (one-off/monthly, designation) or sign up for a kitchen shift.
- **Palette.** Saffron `#F4C33D`, aubergine `#2D1832`, tomato `#E2412B`, milk `#FFF8EC`, leaf `#2F6B47` (availability).
- **Type.** Rubik Variable only: 800–900 display set very large and tight, 400 body at 18–19px, tabular figures for costs.
- **Imagery.** Six CC0 produce/kitchen photos (tomatoes, onions, cabbage, persimmons, bread, stew pot) as hard-edged square tiles, no grading beyond a slight contrast lift.
- **Composition.** Poster hero with two direct actions; "what is in a box" as an itemised receipt plus a cost bar; "where the money goes" as a real four-step sequence; donation panel; volunteer board (kitchen tabs → shift list with places left); "need food?" route; FAQ; footer with kitchen addresses and hours.
- **Signature interaction.** The donation amount is translated live into sample boxes/meals ("40 AZN ≈ 1 box + 1 meal, sample costs").
- **Flow (donate).** Preset or custom amount (5–5 000) → one-off/monthly → designation → name (or anonymous) + e-mail for receipt → review → confirmation "demo — no payment taken, no card details were requested".
- **Flow (volunteer).** Kitchen → shift (full shifts disabled) → name, phone, 16+ confirmation, driver's licence for delivery shifts → confirmation; places left decrement.
- **States.** Custom amount out of range/empty, missing e-mail, full shift, delivery shift without licence, success for both flows.
- **Open issues.** Costs are sample figures (labelled in the interface); kitchens and addresses are fictional.
