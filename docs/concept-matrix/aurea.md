# Aurea — nine rooms in a restored merchant house, Şəki (concept `aurea`, kind `site`)

**Previous problem.** "Bir az da qalın." hero, three generic cards and three room names with no sizes, prices, dates or availability.

**Thesis.** A slow, bookish stay narrative for a small house with a real past: an EB Garamond page set like a printed guest book, wide photographs breaking out of a narrow reading column, and one precise tool — a date-range calendar that turns into a room comparison with nights, availability and totals. The calm comes from typography and proportion, not from "luxury" adjectives.

- **Audience.** Couples and families from Baku and abroad planning 2–4 nights in Şəki; they compare rooms and need practical arrival info.
- **Primary task.** Choose dates → compare rooms for those dates → send an enquiry.
- **Palette.** Lime plaster `#E9E6DF`, paper `#F6F4EF`, walnut ink `#2B2119`, şəbəkə cobalt `#23408E`, saffron `#C9951E`, madder `#8C2A22` (used only for unavailable/error).
- **Type.** EB Garamond only (roman for text, italic for dates and captions, lining tabular figures in the table); Cyrillic covered by the family.
- **Imagery.** Nine CC0 photos (arcaded courtyard, stone lane, valley, terraces, clawfoot bath, three rooms, breakfast). Soft, slightly faded grade in CSS so mixed sources sit together.
- **Composition.** Split hero (courtyard photo / sentence + quick dates), a vertical dated timeline of the house, a rooms comparison table (rows = attributes, columns = rooms) instead of cards, booking calendar, practical "arriving" ledger, FAQ, footer with address and reception hours.
- **Signature interaction.** Two-month range calendar: first click = arrival, second = departure; nights counted live; Friday/Saturday stays need 2 nights; comparison table recalculates totals and remaining rooms per type.
- **Flow.** Dates → guests (adults/children; rooms that cannot sleep the party are disabled) → room → enquiry (name, e-mail or phone, arrival time, optional airport transfer from Qəbələ or Gəncə) → summary with total → confirmation "demo — nothing was sent".
- **States.** No dates yet (empty), too short weekend stay, sold-out room for chosen nights, party too large for a room, contact missing/invalid, success.
- **Content.** House timeline (marked as the concept's fictional history), 4 room types / 9 rooms with m², beds, view, floor, bath and nightly AZN, breakfast hours, parking, transfers, walking distances, cancellation and accessibility FAQ. No reviews or awards.
- **Open issues.** Photos are not from Şəki (CC0 sources from elsewhere), chosen for material and light; captions describe the concept, not the photo location.
