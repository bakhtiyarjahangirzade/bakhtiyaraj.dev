None had working buttons, real content or states. Catalogue previews were abstract SVGs or hand-made mock-up screenshots.

**Verification for every row (merged build, `dist/`):** az/ru/en at 1440×900 and 390×844 (108 page views). ## Shared work
- `storefront/src/k/`: the concept kit. Each concept has its own render module and Vite entry (CSS/JS loaded only on its own page through the build manifest). There are 20 OFL fonts with latin-ext and Cyrillic, a shared ribbon/reset, and `[az, ru, en]` copy leaves checked by `tests/concepts.test.mjs` (CSP rules, local assets, language-prefixed links). The legacy shared renderers (`showroom.js`, `showroom.css`, `concept.css`) were removed.
- Photography: 208 CC0 photographs (WordPress Photo Directory via Openverse or its own API), optimised WebP. Provenance is in `k/<id>/assets.json`, credits are on each page and in `docs/concept-asset-credits.md`, and the files rebuild reproducibly with `scripts/concept-assets/process.py`. Three owner-supplied generated images are reused and captioned.
- Unchanged: concept IDs and URLs, AZ/RU/EN static generation, the sitemap, the ribbon link to the configurator with `reference`, 199/799/1599 AZN and every calculation in `product-data.js`. Only the design preview image paths moved to the real previews. The homepage Dovv shell is unchanged apart from the previews.

## Honest limits
- The photography is amateur CC0 work from many countries. Grades and crops unify it, but it is not a commissioned shoot (weakest: Forma products, Aurea courtyard, Wolfpack's single image). A real client project needs its own photography.
- Prototype state is local, and confirmations say nothing was sent. Some concepts use a fixed demo clock or date; Arena and Sunny Kids read the Baku clock.
- Known small issues are listed per note, for example: Reserve's mobile booking panel sits below the grid, Arena's phone timeline scrolls sideways, Olio's season table scrolls inside its box, and Flow's drag is mouse-only.
